Icons embossed in the glass

Smoked glass. At rest the icons have no colour, just relief in the glass. Hover one and it lights up in its colour as it lifts and its name decodes. Rosie stays lit. "Embossed" could mean two things, so here are both:

A

Raised

The icons stand up out of the glass: lit along the top edge, with a soft shadow underneath.

B

Pressed in

The icons are stamped into the glass: a dark recess, shadowed at the top, with light catching the bottom lip.
