# Provider marks

The bundled SVG marks are LobeHub brand icons, MIT © 2023 LobeHub. The complete grant is retained at `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/LobeIcons-MIT.txt`.

Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Cursor and the local/model-provider marks are copied without modification from Codenotch revision `9156c615bcd75eb50683f047d733bc39738dd7c3`. Its Windows glyph notice identifies `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.0 as the source. Nous Research is fetched directly from that pinned package release. Exact source paths/URL and SHA-256 hashes are in `sources.json`.

Orion follows the reference's OpenAI knot for Codex. Gemini uses the actual Gemini spark (`gemini-alt.svg` upstream), not the Antigravity mark misleadingly named `gemini.svg` in the Windows reference. Monochrome rendering and optical sizing happen in CSS; the SVG files are unchanged. Unknown custom endpoints use their configured-name initials, never a falsely attributed provider logo.

These marks identify their respective products. Trademark rights remain with the respective owners; the icon library's MIT license does not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement.
